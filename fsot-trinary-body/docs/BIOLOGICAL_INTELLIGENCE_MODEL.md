### Genome → Formal exam field (expression)

Exam load is **not** a free weight. On each item:

1. Refresh `genome_to_guidance` (codon dual-axis → δψ, D_eff, emergence, **domain_coupling**).
2. Map suite/subject → Formal domain (`item_fsot_domain`).
3. Blend **heritable base** × (1 − w) with **Formal field** × w, where  
   `w ∝ domain_coupling` (genetic expression of how hard the field binds).
4. `compute(S)` under that field → regime / pressure / CHEW depth.

So math/science competence is **genetic Formal expression under environment**, not a leaderboard patch.
